How it works

Three parties. You own the one in the middle.

Claude talks to your server. Your server talks to your bank through Enable Banking. Nobody else.

01

Deploy your own copy

One container on Railway, Fly or your own box. A fresh server shows a setup page. No terminal.

02

Register at Enable Banking

Free. You get a private key that never leaves your server. The setup page shows every value their form asks for.

03

Connect Claude and your bank

Add your server as a connector, sign in once, then say connect my bank and log in at the bank as usual.

What you can ask

Plain questions, real numbers.

The same balances and transactions your banking app shows.

  • What's my balance?
  • How much did we spend on groceries in May?
  • Make me a monthly budget from the last three months.
  • Which subscriptions am I paying for, and what do they cost a year?
  • Has the rent gone out?
  • Tell me if my balance drops below 5,000.
  • Build me a spending dashboard for August.
  • Anything unusual this month?
Yours

Yours, start to finish.

Looks, never touches

Read-only by design. There are no payment tools, so there is nothing to worry about.

Lives where you put it

Runs on your server with your key. Nobody in between.

Remembers nothing

Every answer is fetched fresh from the bank. Nothing is kept.

Easy to walk away from

Change the password or revoke at your bank, and that's that. No account to close.

Open source

Software you run. Not a service you join.

BankMCP™ is a repo, not a company. You deploy your own copy and it answers only to you. MIT licence.

Prefer no AI vendor at all? It also runs with a local model through Ollama, so a transaction never leaves your machine except to reach your bank. Early days and slower, but it works. Recipe in the README.

BankMCP™ is not a bank and gives no financial advice. Independent of Enable Banking, Anthropic and any bank. MIT, as is.
