How it works

Three parties, and you own the one in the middle. Claude talks to your server. Your server talks to your bank through Enable Banking, a licensed open-banking provider covering 2,700+ European banks.

1

Deploy your own copy

One container, one small volume, on Railway, Fly or your own box. A fresh server shows a setup page; no terminal needed.

2

Register at Enable Banking

Free. You get an application and a private key that never leaves your server. The setup page shows every value their form asks for.

3

Connect Claude and your bank

Add your server as a connector, sign in once with your password, then say “connect my bank” and log in at the bank as usual.

What you can ask

Plain questions, answered from the real numbers. Claude sees the same balances and transactions your banking app shows.

  • What's my balance?
  • How much did we spend on groceries in May?
  • Which subscriptions am I paying for, and what do they cost per year?
  • Has the rent gone out?
  • Tell me if my balance drops below 5,000.
  • What have I paid the cleaner on MobilePay this year?

It never moves money.

Bank™ is built so the worst case is boring.

Read-only, by design

There are no payment tools and there never will be. Bank consents are read-only, and the server has no code path that could initiate a transfer.

Your server, your key

The private key and the bank consents live on a server only you control. No third party, no shared service, no account to sign up for.

Nothing stored

Balances and transactions are fetched when you ask and never written to disk. The only state is which banks you connected and your login.

One password, one alarm, one kill switch

Every sign-in is logged and can message you. Changing the password logs every client out. Revoking at your bank ends it all.
