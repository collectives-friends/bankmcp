How it works

Three parties. You own the one in the middle.

Claude talks to your server. Your server talks to your bank through Enable Banking, a licensed open-banking provider. Nobody else is in the chain.

01

Deploy your own copy

One container and a small volume, on Railway, Fly or your own box. A fresh server shows a setup page in the browser. No terminal needed.

02

Register at Enable Banking

Free. You get an application and a private key that never leaves your server. The setup page shows every value their form asks for, with copy buttons.

03

Connect Claude and your bank

Add your server as a connector in Claude, sign in once with your password, then say connect my bank and log in at the bank as usual.

What you can ask

Plain questions, real numbers.

Claude sees the same balances and transactions your banking app shows, and nothing it doesn't.

  • What's my balance?
  • How much did we spend on groceries in May?
  • Which subscriptions am I paying for, and what do they cost a year?
  • Has the rent gone out?
  • Tell me if my balance drops below 5,000.
  • Build me a spending dashboard for August.
  • Anything unusual this month?

Security

It never moves money. The worst case is boring.

Read-only, by design

There are no payment tools and there never will be. Bank consents are read-only, and the server has no code path that could initiate a transfer.

Your server, your key

The private key and the bank consents live on a server only you control. No third party, no shared service, no account to sign up for.

Nothing stored

Balances and transactions are fetched when you ask and never written to disk. The only state is which banks you connected and your login.

One password, one alarm, one kill switch

Every sign-in is logged and can message you. Changing the password logs every client out. Revoking the consent at your bank ends it all.

Software, not a service

There is no hosted Bank™. That's the point.

Each person deploys their own copy and is its only operator: they register their own Enable Banking application, accept its terms themselves, and are responsible for their own hosting, password and security. The authors publish the code and nothing else, receive no data, and are not affiliated with Enable Banking, Anthropic or any bank.

Bank™ is not a bank. It holds no money and gives no financial advice. The ™ is a joke; the read-only part is not. Provided as is, without warranty, under the MIT licence.
